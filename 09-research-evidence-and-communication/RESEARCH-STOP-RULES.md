# Research Stop Rules

**Status:** 🔴 RED — rules drafted; practical thresholds need validation.

## Purpose
Keep research bounded, useful, and evidence-driven instead of repeating low-value searches.

## Procedure
1. Define the exact question and what evidence would answer it.
2. Search the highest-value authoritative sources first.
3. Record query/source coverage and key findings.
4. If results conflict, stale sources dominate, or a key fact is missing, change the query or source class.
5. For troubleshooting, follow Section 06's distinct-attempt and broaden-research rules.
6. Stop when acceptance criteria are met, additional sources are unlikely to change the decision, or the authorized time/scope is exhausted.
7. If unresolved, report what was checked, what remains unknown, and the smallest next research step.

## Guardrails
Do not claim exhaustive research without defining and checking the search scope. Do not use snippets alone for material claims. Do not contact third parties or transmit sensitive data without required approval.

## Dependencies
Sections 05–06, 08, 10, and 13.

## Acceptance evidence
A research note shows a clear question, meaningful source coverage, a justified stop decision, and explicit unresolved gaps.
