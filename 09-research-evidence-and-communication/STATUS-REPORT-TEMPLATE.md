## Reporting rules
Use explicit outcomes for each material item: PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, NOT CHECKED, or NOT APPLICABLE with rationale. Keep status claims aligned with the root/section status definitions. Include enough evidence to recheck the claim without exposing secrets.

## Reporting rules
Separate completion from validation. Never imply external communication, deployment, integration, or persistence succeeded without confirmation. Report meaningful failures and limitations. Keep the report concise enough to act on but complete enough to audit.
