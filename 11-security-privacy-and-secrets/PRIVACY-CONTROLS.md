# Privacy Controls

**Status:** 🔴 RED — control framework drafted; purpose and jurisdiction review pending.

## Purpose
Minimize privacy risk across collection, use, access, retention, sharing, and deletion of personal data.

## Requirements
- Define purpose and necessity before collecting personal data.
- Minimize fields, retention duration, and access scope.
- Explain relevant data use to users in clear language where applicable.
- Identify storage locations, processors, external recipients, and cross-border transfers.
- Set retention and deletion rules, including backups and derived data where feasible.
- Support appropriate access, correction, export, or deletion processes where required.
- Avoid collecting sensitive information in logs and telemetry unless strictly necessary and authorized.
- Apply privacy-by-design to analytics, support, AI processing, and third-party integrations.
- Assess applicable legal and contractual requirements for the actual jurisdictions and user groups; do not assume one jurisdiction's rules cover all cases.

## Change review
Reassess privacy impact when data types, purposes, recipients, providers, retention, or user populations change. Obtain required approval before material disclosure or policy changes.

## Dependencies
Sections 01–03, 05, 09–10, and data classification.

## Acceptance evidence
A real project has a data inventory, purpose/retention rules, access controls, and reviewed deletion/disclosure workflows.
