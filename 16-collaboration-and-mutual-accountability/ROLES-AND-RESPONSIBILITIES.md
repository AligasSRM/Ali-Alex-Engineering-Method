# Roles and Responsibilities

**Status:** RED — role model drafted; project-specific assignment pending.

## Purpose
Make responsibility and authority explicit without assuming that one person or agent can approve every action.

## Role fields
- Stable role/assignment ID and lifecycle state:
- Named primary owner and backup/fallback owner:
- Scope of authority, approval limits, and environments covered:
- Evidence of access grant and review/expiry date where applicable:
- Role name and purpose.
- Responsibilities and deliverables.
- Decision authority and explicit limits.
- Required skills/access and separation-of-duty constraints.
- Escalation contact or fallback owner.
- Evidence of assignment and review cadence.

## Typical roles
Product/request owner, implementation owner, reviewer, security/privacy reviewer, release approver, operations owner, and external provider owner. One person may hold multiple roles in a small project only where risk and approval rules permit.

## Rules
Map each role to the relevant Section 05 approval category and Section 11 access-control boundary. Define incompatible duties and whether self-review/self-approval is prohibited for each risk class. If a required role is unassigned, treat the affected decision as blocked rather than assuming authority.

## Rules
Responsibility does not imply authority to perform high-impact actions. Approval requirements in Section 05 remain controlling. Do not grant access merely because a role is named.

## Dependencies
Sections 02, 05, 09–13, and 17.

## Acceptance evidence
Each critical responsibility and approval path has a named authorized owner or an explicit unresolved assignment.
