## Resolution steps
1. State the disputed claim or decision precisely.
2. Separate observable evidence from interpretation and preference.
3. Identify the requirement, risk, and decision authority involved.
4. Compare options against explicit criteria and documented constraints.
5. Seek additional evidence or a bounded experiment when uncertainty is material.
6. Record the decision, dissent, rationale, and revisit trigger.
7. Escalate unresolved high-impact disputes to the authorized decision-maker.

## Guardrails
If the decision authority is unavailable or the dispute concerns a mandatory legal/security/privacy control, do not proceed by majority preference or silence; keep the affected action blocked and escalate through the authorized route.
Do not resolve disagreement through repetition, personal attacks, silent scope changes, or unsupported certainty. A decision owner may choose among acceptable options but cannot waive mandatory legal, safety, security, or approval constraints without a valid authorized path.
