# Deferred Work

**Status:** 🔴 RED — deferral policy drafted; project backlog review pending.

## Required record
- Stable deferral ID and lifecycle: PROPOSED / APPROVED / REJECTED / REOPENED / CLOSED.
- Item, reason, and original requirement.
- Evidence explaining why it is not addressed now, alternatives considered, and date reviewed.
- Impact, affected users/components, and residual risk.
- Whether release or acceptance is blocked.
- Explicit decision-maker, approval ID, scope, and expiry/review date where required.
- Compensating controls, if any.
- Re-entry trigger, owner, and review date.
- Tests or acceptance criteria required when resumed.

## Rules
Every deferral must state whether it blocks acceptance or release, who owns the residual risk, what signal reopens the work, and which tests/controls are required at re-entry. Expired deferrals must be re-approved or reopened; they do not auto-renew.

## Rules
Deferral is a visible decision, not a claim of completion. Do not defer mandatory security, privacy, legal, safety, or data-integrity controls without an authorized and lawful resolution. Reassess deferred items when dependencies, risk, scope, or requirements change.
