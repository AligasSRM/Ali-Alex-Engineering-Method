Cross-link rather than duplicating entries. Record the exact repository, branch, commit, evidence location, reviewer, and lifecycle state for material claims.

## Guardrail
Conversation history alone is not proof that a change is committed or saved to the intended repository. Distinguish drafted, saved, committed, tested, deployed, and released states.
