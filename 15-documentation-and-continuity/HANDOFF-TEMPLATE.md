## Rules
A handoff is information, not permission. The receiving operator must verify the live repository and current authorization; any material mismatch blocks planned changes until reconciled. Keep the next action singular and bounded.

## Rules
Do not report unverified work as complete. Do not include passwords, tokens, recovery codes, or unnecessary personal information. Make the next action specific and safe.
