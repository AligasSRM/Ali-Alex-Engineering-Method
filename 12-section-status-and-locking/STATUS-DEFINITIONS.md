# Status Definitions

**Status:** 🔴 RED — definitions drafted; full-method consistency review pending.

## RED
Structure or work is incomplete, material requirements are unmet, evidence is missing, or a blocker remains.

## YELLOW
Work is actively in progress with defined scope, owner, and next action. It does not imply acceptance.

## ORANGE
Work is paused, blocked, awaiting a dependency, or requires a decision. Document the blocker and safe next action.

## GREEN
All applicable acceptance criteria are satisfied and supported by reviewed evidence. Limitations and non-blocking residual risks are documented.

## LOCKED
GREEN has passed final review and relevant regression checks; the reviewed scope is frozen against casual change. LOCKED does not prevent justified reopening.

## Evidence freshness and scope
A status applies only to the named scope and reviewed version/commit. Re-evaluate affected criteria when source files, dependencies, configuration, environment, requirements, or test results change. Preserve prior status history; do not overwrite the reason for an earlier decision. If evidence is unavailable or stale, use RED or ORANGE according to whether work is incomplete or blocked.

## Rules
- Every status identifies scope and supporting evidence.
- Use the least optimistic status consistent with the facts.
- A label never substitutes for test output, review, or approval.
- Keep status language consistent across section records and reports.
