# Decisions

**Status:** 🔴 RED — no substantive decisions approved yet.

Record each decision using the following fields:
- Stable decision ID, proposed date, and last-updated date.
- Decision statement and lifecycle state: `PROPOSED`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `DEFERRED`, or `SUPERSEDED`.
- Context, user/business need, and evidence/source references.
- Facts distinguished from assumptions and hypotheses.
- Options considered, including a reason for rejecting reasonable alternatives.
- Rationale, trade-offs, consequences, and known risks.
- Accountable owner and authorized approver; approval evidence/date.
- Affected requirement IDs in `02-planning-before-implementation/REQUIREMENT-TRACEABILITY-MATRIX.md`.
- Affected sections/files, dependencies, tests, and release implications.
- Revisit trigger or review date; superseding decision ID if applicable.
