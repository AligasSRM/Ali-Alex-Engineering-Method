# Success Criteria

**Status:** 🔴 RED — metrics pending definition.

## Candidate measurement dimensions
- User task completion and time-on-task.
- Learnability and usability.
- Reliability and recovery from failure.
- Accessibility and responsive experience.
- Data integrity, privacy, and security.
- Operational cost and maintainability.
- Adoption and user-reported value, where measurable.

## Required for each metric
Define the metric precisely (including numerator/denominator or timing rules where applicable), baseline or comparison method, target and rationale, measurement procedure, evidence source, owner, review point, and limitations. Do not invent targets before the product scope and baseline are understood. Avoid vanity metrics that do not represent user or operational outcomes.
