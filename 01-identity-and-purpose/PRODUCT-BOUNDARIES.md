## Scope-change rule
A material scope change must identify affected requirements, design/files, dependencies, cost/schedule, security/privacy, tests, and release impact. Follow Section 05 approval and change-control rules before implementation.

## Boundary rules
- Distinguish essential first-release capabilities from future opportunities.
- Record cross-section dependencies.
- Require explicit approval for material scope changes.
- Do not treat an idea, mockup, or planned integration as a working capability.
