## Decision rules
- Start from verified requirements and constraints, not trend or familiarity alone.
- Compare viable alternatives, support lifecycle, security posture, accessibility, ecosystem, cost, and operational burden as applicable.
- Record why an option was rejected and what evidence could trigger reconsideration.
- Prefer supported, maintainable technologies; avoid unnecessary novelty and lock-in.
- Verify current vendor/project documentation before final selection.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Each major choice maps to a requirement or constraint.
- [ ] Alternatives and trade-offs are documented.
- [ ] Support status and source evidence are current.
- [ ] Decision owner and review date are recorded.
- [ ] No choice is described as approved without explicit approval.
