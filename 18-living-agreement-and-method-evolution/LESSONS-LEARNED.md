# Lessons Learned

**Status:** RED — record template drafted; verified project lessons not yet collected.

## Required fields
- Stable lesson ID and lifecycle: OBSERVED / UNDER INVESTIGATION / VALIDATED / ACCEPTED / REJECTED / DEFERRED / CLOSED.
- Lesson ID and date.
- Project, stage, and affected sections.
- Observation, evidence IDs/links, source timestamp, and exact project/commit/environment scope.
- Impact and who/what was affected.
- Root-cause confidence and alternative explanations not ruled out.
- What worked and what did not.
- Immediate correction, if any.
- Proposed method change and expected result.
- Proposed change ID, decision/approval ID, validation plan, accountable owner, and review date.
- Follow-up outcome evidence, unintended effects, revisit trigger, and closure authority.

## Quality rules
Separate observation from interpretation. Do not present an unverified hypothesis as a root cause. Avoid blame and redact secrets or unnecessary personal information. Link to evidence instead of copying sensitive artifacts.

## Dependencies
Sections 06–09, 12–16, and the evolution policy.

## Acceptance evidence
Each material lesson has evidence, an owner or decision, and a clear disposition: accepted, rejected, deferred, or still under investigation.
